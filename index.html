Why folks come back

Clean, wholesome, and full of flavor.

The Turkey House is a one-man-strong food truck with a big idea: good food shouldn’t cost you your health. Everything on the menu is turkey, seasoned and marinated for days, then smoked, grilled or fried fresh for your order.

Our recipes stay secret. Our kitchen doesn’t. We keep it clean, we keep it honest, and we want you to come back and bring a friend.

How ordering works

  1. Build your order.
    Pick your food, pick a pickup time. It takes about a minute.
  2. We cook it fresh.
    Nothing sits under a heat lamp. Your food is made when you’re on your way.
  3. Walk up, or stay in the car.
    Tell us your car when you order and we’ll wave you over when it’s ready.

Prefer to call?

Phone orders are welcome. Call (984) 314-6485 and have your card ready. We take payment when you order so your food is hot and waiting.
